## Earlier Champions balance decisions

Divergence adopts the following April and June changes selectively. The release already contains the upstream April and June mechanics under Champions configurations. [April mechanics #10151](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/pull/10151) · [June mechanics #10257](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/pull/10257).

- **Already present:** Salt Cure’s 1/16 residual damage, or 1/8 against Water and Steel. The local defaults also have 1-in-8 full paralysis, 25% freeze thaw and a guaranteed thaw on the third attempted turn, and sleep with a 1-in-3 wake chance on turn two and a guaranteed wake on turn three. Divergence’s standard freeze application already resets the thaw counter, the behavior covered by [Expansion’s later freeze initialization fix](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/pull/11095).
- **Battle rules implemented:** Unseen Fist now deals quarter damage through protection and activates contact shields. Rage Fist retains its stacks on switching but resets on fainting. Make It Rain stays at 100% accuracy and drops the user’s Sp. Atk by two stages once per use. Encore uses the forced move’s priority, and Speed Swap survives Mega Evolution.
- **Move data implemented:** Gear Grind 60 BP per hit and 90% accuracy, Anchor Shot 90 BP, Revelation Dance and Dragon Hammer 100, Triple Dive 35 per hit, Hyper Drill 120, Bolt Beak and Fishious Rend 80, Astral Barrage 110, Blood Moon 130. Metal Claw and Dire Claw count as slicing moves. Freeze-Dry loses its secondary status effect and Sheer Force eligibility. Clangorous Soul uses always-hit accuracy.
- **Adventure PP decisions:** Night Slash gets 20 base PP, Shell Trap and Spin Out get 10. Protect, Sandstorm, King’s Shield, Spiky Shield, Baneful Bunker, Obstruct and Snowscape stay at 10, Purify at 20 and Beak Blast at 15.
- **Selection comparison:** After their prerequisites fail, Divergence permits selecting Fake Out, First Impression, Spit Up, Burn Up, Double Shock and Last Resort. It blocks Belch before eating a Berry and Stuff Cheeks without a held Berry. Champions reverses each selection rule. Reviewed only; a change to menu behavior remains a separate decision.
- **Contact KO comparison:** Against both Rough Skin and Iron Barbs, Knock Off removes the item, Thief steals it, Rapid Spin clears entry hazards and Ice Spinner clears terrain even when the attacker faints. Magician does not steal. Those outcomes match Champions. Divergence also sets hazards with Ceaseless Edge and Stone Axe in that situation, a remaining timing difference. No timing port was selected.
- **Launch data already aligned:** Grass-type Growth, Steel-type Snap Trap, Crabhammer and Syrup Bomb accuracy, the slicing flags on Crush Claw, Dragon Claw and Shadow Claw, the sound flag on Dragon Cheer, Iron Head, Moonblast and Dire Claw secondary chances, Toxic Thread’s two-stage Speed drop, and the BP changes to Bone Rush, Night Daze, First Impression, Spirit Shackle, Fire Lash, Trop Kick, Beak Blast, Apple Acid, Grav Apple, Psyshield Bash, Mountain Gale and Infernal Parade. [Expansion launch data diff](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/pull/10058/files).
- **Keep as a Divergence decision:** Snipe Shot is deliberately reworked to 100 BP and 80% accuracy, and supports Inteleon’s Take Aim. Champions uses 85 BP.
