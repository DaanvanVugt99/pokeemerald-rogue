## Earlier Champions parity still open

These are separate balance decisions for Divergence. The release already contains the upstream April and June mechanics under Champions configurations. [April mechanics #10151](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/pull/10151) · [June mechanics #10257](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/pull/10257).

- **Already present:** Salt Cure’s 1/16 residual damage, or 1/8 against Water and Steel. The local defaults also have 1-in-8 full paralysis, 25% freeze thaw and a guaranteed thaw on the third attempted turn, and sleep with a 1-in-3 wake chance on turn two and a guaranteed wake on turn three. Divergence’s standard freeze application already resets the thaw counter, the behavior covered by [Expansion’s later freeze initialization fix](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/pull/11095).
- **Confirmed differences:** Rage Fist retains its stack on switching in Divergence. Champions clears it on switching and fainting. Local Make It Rain is 100% accurate and drops the user’s Sp. Atk one stage; Champions uses 95% and a two-stage drop. Local Unseen Fist bypasses Protect at full damage for contact moves; Champions uses quarter damage and applies contact-shield effects. Piercing Drill already uses quarter damage and triggers the shield effects locally.
- **Mechanics to compare with focused tests:** Encore replacing the chosen move’s priority during the same turn, Speed Swap surviving Mega Evolution, and move-selection rules for Fake Out, First Impression, Spit Up, Burn Up, Double Shock, Last Resort, Belch and Stuff Cheeks. Local Belch and Stuff Cheeks have selection restrictions. Also compare Rough Skin and Iron Barbs KO timing for Knock Off, Rapid Spin, Ice Spinner and Thief, with Ceaseless Edge, Stone Axe and Magician as negative controls.
- **Confirmed launch move-data differences:** Protect, Sandstorm, King’s Shield, Spiky Shield, Baneful Bunker, Obstruct and Snowscape 10 → 5 base PP; Night Slash 15 → 20; Purify 20 → 5; Beak Blast 15 → 5; Shell Trap and Spin Out 5 → 10. Gear Grind 50 → 60 BP and 85% → 90% accuracy. Anchor Shot 80 → 90 BP, Revelation Dance and Dragon Hammer 90 → 100, Bolt Beak and Fishious Rend 85 → 80, Astral Barrage 120 → 110, Triple Dive 30 → 35, Hyper Drill 100 → 120, Blood Moon 140 → 130. Metal Claw and Dire Claw gain slicing; Freeze-Dry loses its 10% freeze or frostbite chance; Clangorous Soul changes from accuracy 100 to the always-hit setting.
- **Launch data already aligned:** Grass-type Growth, Steel-type Snap Trap, Crabhammer and Syrup Bomb accuracy, the slicing flags on Crush Claw, Dragon Claw and Shadow Claw, the sound flag on Dragon Cheer, Iron Head, Moonblast and Dire Claw secondary chances, Toxic Thread’s two-stage Speed drop, and the BP changes to Bone Rush, Night Daze, First Impression, Spirit Shackle, Fire Lash, Trop Kick, Beak Blast, Apple Acid, Grav Apple, Psyshield Bash, Mountain Gale and Infernal Parade. [Expansion launch data diff](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/pull/10058/files).
- **Keep as a Divergence decision:** Snipe Shot is deliberately reworked to 100 BP and 80% accuracy, and supports Inteleon’s Take Aim. Champions uses 85 BP. Protect-family PP and the Rage Fist and Unseen Fist nerfs are the other high-impact balance decisions for an Adventure run.

## Suggested implementation order

1. Mega Golisopod’s regular Ability update is implemented locally.
2. September power, punching flag and Mega Sol updates implemented. The September PP and learnset decisions are settled above.
3. December move data and tutor additions implemented. Verify Stalwart, Fire Boost and both new Mega assignments against the live version, then check the remaining M-D forms.
4. Review the older balance differences and tackle move ordering, pivot and revival mechanics as independent changes. Expansion puts its battle rules behind config flags, and Divergence’s battle engine and Uniques warrant selective ports with focused tests.
