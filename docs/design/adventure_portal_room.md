# Adventure Gate: underground portal room

The first hub district reskin replaces `Rogue_Area_AdventureEntrance` with a 20x16
research chamber with a uniformly black exterior void. Its side walls now sit at
x=2 and x=16: 13 floor columns centered on the gateway and console at x=9.
The south passage spans x=8..10 with jambs at x=7 and x=11, sharing that centerline.
The retained map buffer and short access corridors preserve existing hub connections. Other districts and their shared tilesets
are unchanged. This is a console-controlled portal room, not an NPC-controlled exit.

## Layout and interaction

The upright gateway spans x=7..11, y=0..3. Its three lower opening tiles are walkable;
walking there outside the departure script gives directions and steps the player
back. None of these tiles has warp behavior. Adventure setup is the only departure path.

The dedicated console is object 11 at (9,6), approachable only from (9,7). Its
surrounding equipment blocks side/back access so the departure starts reliably.
It has a dedicated PC-derived sprite with dark grey casing and a black miniature setup
menu (warm heading, pale option rows and cursor), separate from the blue storage PC.
The two flanking controls use the same dark grey casing through a private foreground
palette; their indicator colors and underlying floor stay intact.
The sprite reuses NPC_4, retains the original silhouette and appends a graphics ID;
its source is generated by the room recipe without changing shared PC art.
Once preparation, party/daycare checks and commit succeed, the player walks right
twice, up three times, left twice, then up to (9,3). This routes around the controls
and uses the existing portal transition at the center of the opening.
Starter selection happens at the console; cancelling it needs no return walk.
The first-run intro state advances only after validation succeeds.

The points of interest form three groups around the central adventure console:

| Group | Positions | Placement rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team preparation | Storage PC (5,5) | Near the console, with its own space beneath the west equipment bank. |
| Hub progress | Quest board (5,8), nurse (7,8), workbench (5,11) | Guidance beside the board; hub-upgrade spending immediately below quest/reward services. The workbench is a hub-construction service, not run crafting. |
| Special adventures | Peonia (12,5), replay flag (14,5), Trial guide (12,8) | Peonia and her switch stay visibly paired; Trial guidance sits near the console where Trials are selected. |

The nurse and Trial guide frame the approach at x=7 and x=12 without occupying
its center. The replay pair has enough horizontal clearance for the flag's 32px
sprite. Every service is approachable from below, including the workbench (which
rejects interaction from behind). The lower-priority workbench stays on the edge,
out of the direct storage-to-console path.

The entire y=10 story crossing and x=9 arrival aisle from y=7..12 stay open.
The south opening extends the aisle through y=13 when that connection is built.
The generator checks front-of-service access in all eight connection states,
including with every optional service present. Structural collision symmetry is
checked about x=9 whenever west/east connectivity matches. Fresh-game previews omit locked
services so the main console, storage and nurse/board remain easy to identify.

Return/teleport warp 6 remains at (9,7). The nurse retains quest-log, progression,
package and return responsibilities; her tutorial now refers to the board beside
her. The first post-win console use introduces Ascension. Moving services reloads
older entrance snapshots, including the moved replay pair, without a save break.

## Setup and session rules

Begin Adventure remains the first setup row. Trial appears second after its service
upgrade, defaults to None, and is hidden in hub-only editors and replay setup.
The picker retains grouped Trials, ascension/format eligibility and legal dex choices.
None rebuilds an ordinary context from remembered ordinary settings. Opening the
picker preserves accepted ordinary settings; cancelling the picker retains the
previous context. Closing the console clears its pending Trial and config override.
Trial overrides are never written over ordinary remembered settings.
Peonia and the flag still choose a replay; its console setup remains read-only.

## Tile provenance and generation

Run `python scripts/generate_portal_room.py` with Pillow installed. Use `--check`
to compare all generated sources and validate all eight connection masks.
The main map and border in `data/layouts/Rogue_Area_AdventureEntrance/` are now
authored in Porymap. The script reads them for previews and validation and never
rewrites them. The user's extended yellow aisle markings at x=7/x=11 are preserved.
The script still generates the private tilesets, console sprite and corridor facades;
source IDs are recorded in `data/tilesets/portal_room_sources.json`. Edit that recipe
for asset changes. Keep entrance geometry changes synchronized with the closed-exit
fills in `src/rogue_hub.c`, map events and the private corridor facades.

The room is now assembled from the existing `Rogue_Encounter_GalacticHQ` layout,
not from guessed neighboring atlas entries. Its Facility/Galactic graphics and
palettes remain paired. The generator records the donor coordinates for each tile
and the source/destination rectangles for complete furniture assemblies.

Verified reference arrangements (zero-based coordinates):

- North wall: HQ (4,1) upper wall, (4,2) face, (1,4) floor shadow;
  regular floor at (3,4).
- Lower walls: HQ row 11 black backing and exposed pillar caps, followed by
  upper wall row 12 and face row 13. The room repeats that complete section at
  y=13..15, leaving only the south opening through its black backing.
- Outer pillar: (0,0) cap, (0,1) join, (0,4) shaft, (0,8) end.
  Lower wall-break caps use the black-backed (5,11) variant, not the floor-backed
  exposed passage start at (2,8).
- Inward tunnel join: (5,12), (5,13), (5,14), including its floor-facing foot.
- Generator banks: complete 2x3 rectangle at (1,1), copied to (4,1) and (13,1).
- Wall displays: complete 1x2 rectangle at (11,1).
- Console controls: identical 1x2 rectangles from (6,2), placed at (8,5) and (10,5).
  Their foreground remains intact; only the shadowed background layer beneath
  their feet is replaced by the regular room floor.
- Console floor markings: walkable perimeter tiles from the Poke Ball Factory
  at (1,2) and (12,2), placed at (8,7) and (10,7). Their normal floor remains
  traversable; these are the original yellow markings without the factory railings.
- Gateway frame: the doorway at (1,12)-(3,13); its center/jamb runs extend
  around the custom animated opening. This extension is specific to our gateway.

Donor palettes remain unchanged for the chamber and rear equipment. Four additional
private palette banks shade the exit floors to 80%, 60%, 40% and 20% brightness,
quantized to GBA RGB555. Eight-pixel bands lead into uniformly dark facade floors,
following the outdoor hub's entrance-shadow and dark-floor (GeneralHub 0x045)
arrangement. West/east fades mirror each other; south fades downwards. These
shades change neither collision nor directional warp behavior. Twelve of thirteen
available palette banks are used, including the portal and side-control palettes.
Exterior borders, wall backing and private facade backgrounds are solid black.
The original mixed lab grid, terrain runner, guessed furniture halves, and cave
edges directly touching laboratory floor have been removed. Decorative donor
behaviors are cleared; complete imported furniture retains donor collision
footprints except where the console needs explicitly solid side controls.

The opening is an original procedural 48x48 pixel animation. Concentric purple
bands contract toward a dark center while red arcs rotate through them. Eight
cyclic phases advance every eight game frames, for a roughly one-second loop.
The texture remains indexed to one 16-color GBA palette and occupies the first
36 secondary tiles. The frame and surrounding machinery remain static.
`gate-animated.gif` previews the loop and `gate-phases.png` shows its eight phases.

The generator writes previews under `build/portal_room/`: `room.png` contains the
assembled tiles; `room-events.png` overlays service labels. `room-inhabited.png`
and `room-native.png` include actual service sprites with their runtime palettes.
`room-first-visit.png` shows the initial always-visible services.
`reference-hq.png` renders the source room. `room-context.png` and the `camera-*`
previews include corridor facades and 240x160 camera crops, so visible seams can
be reviewed at the same viewport size as the game. Optional services are shown
unlocked; temporary story actors are omitted from these art previews.
`connection-variants.png` shows all eight connectivity states, and
`room-east-closed.png` specifically previews the continuous wall at a closed east exit.

## Connections, reloads and validation

Original west/east/south warp indices and positions remain unchanged. Appended
indices 7/8 cover the upper west/east tunnel lane at y=10 (also used by story
entrances). These two added warps resolve through the original west/east
connection destinations; otherwise this walkable row could bypass the warp and
enter a facade map. The lower y=13 lane is now solid black wall backing, so its
previously appended lateral warp entries are removed. New index 9 covers the
third south lane at (8,15), resolving through original south warp 2. Original warp
indices remain intact.
Closed exits seal all three lateral lanes. Private corridor facades
use compatible tiles and retain existing connection offsets and facade footprints
(16x8 horizontal, 8x12 vertical). Their open strips align with the warp lanes. The west buffer is one tile shorter,
so its facade supplies the final dark floor band at the mirrored distance from
the room; the south facade is three tiles wide. Closed lateral exits restore a
continuous side wall and black exterior; a closed south exit restores the complete
bottom wall. Doorway strips are not stacked to simulate a wall. The outdoor common path
fixup is skipped. MAP_TYPE_INDOOR / WEATHER_NONE suppress weather and seasonal tint.

No save fields or format version change. Map/layout entries are appended, preserving
old IDs. Loading an entrance snapshot with a missing console or stale fixed-service
positions/graphics enables the existing continue-game heal warp to rebuild its
objects and camera at the safe return point. Comparing counts alone was incorrect:
a follower could make an old snapshot have the same count. The destination and
continue-warp status must both be set.
Current snapshots continue normally. Hub tiles already reload from source on continue.

Focused tests cover Trial availability, ordinary config preservation, cancellation
cleanup, console departure position, all connection masks and both Trial unlock
states. The generator validates capacities and reachability. In-game checks still
need to cover fresh introduction, ordinary/Trial/replay departures, starter and save
cancellation, return rewards, followers, postgame scenes and each tunnel direction.
